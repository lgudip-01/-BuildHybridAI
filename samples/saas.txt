SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Agreement is effective 2026-05-01 between Halcyon Cloud Systems GmbH ("Provider") and Verdant Logistics B.V. ("Customer").

1. Subscription. Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive right to use the Halcyon Fleet platform for up to 250 named users during the Term.

2. Fees. Customer will pay EUR 84,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

3. Service Levels. Provider will make the platform available 99.9% of each calendar month. If it misses this target, Customer receives a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee.

4. Data Protection. Provider processes personal data only on Customer's documented instructions and in line with the GDPR. Provider's data protection officer is Johanna Weiss (dpo@halcyon-cloud.de).

5. Limitation of Liability. Except for breaches of confidentiality, each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

6. Term and Termination. The initial Term is 36 months and renews for 12-month periods unless either party gives 90 days' notice.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Netherlands, and the courts of Amsterdam have exclusive jurisdiction.
