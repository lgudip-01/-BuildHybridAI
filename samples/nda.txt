MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2026 by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Northwind"), and Bellhaven Robotics LLC, a California limited liability company ("Bellhaven").

1. Purpose. The parties wish to evaluate a possible joint venture (the "Purpose"). Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other for the Purpose only.

2. Obligations. The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own, and no less than reasonable care. Northwind's obligations survive for 3 years after termination.

3. Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party.

4. Notices. Notices to Northwind go to Priya Raman, General Counsel, at legal@northwind-analytics.com or 1180 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 400, Oakland, CA 94607. Notices to Bellhaven go to Mr. Tomas Lindqvist at (415) 555-0142.

5. Remedies. A breach may cause irreparable harm, and the disclosing party may seek injunctive relief. BELLHAVEN acknowledges that damages alone may not be adequate.

6. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
